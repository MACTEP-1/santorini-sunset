Privacy Policy — Santorini Sunset

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Santorini Sunset is a watch face for Garmin Connect IQ devices. This page describes what data it collects and how it is used.

Data collection

Santorini Sunset does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All information it displays — steps, heart rate, battery level, weather, and similar stats — is read directly from your Garmin device's own on-device APIs, shown on screen, and never saved or sent anywhere. The app has no server, no account system, and no analytics. The background image bundled with the app is a static image included in the app itself, not something collected from you.

Location (Positioning permission)

Santorini Sunset requests the Positioning permission for a single purpose: calculating local sunrise and sunset times for the optional Sunrise and Sunset watch-face fields. Your location is read transiently from your device (or, as a fallback, from your Garmin Connect app's synced weather station location) only at the moment this calculation runs. It is never stored, logged, or transmitted to any server or third party by this app.

Third parties

Santorini Sunset does not include any advertising, analytics, or tracking software development kits. No data of any kind is shared with third parties.

Children's privacy

Santorini Sunset is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age — because it does not collect data from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL.
